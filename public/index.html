Collaborative Priority Species List
for Reforestation in New York

New York has over 1.7 million acres of land suitable for reforestation. This page summarizes a collaborative list of 40 priority tree species intended to help practitioners and nurseries coordinate on the species most likely to be needed.

40 species • 20 genera
Prioritized with a multi-criteria framework
Balances ecological + social + practical needs

Why this list exists

A major obstacle to scaling reforestation is the limited commercial availability of native seedlings. This list is designed to help create clearer demand signals for nurseries and support landscape-scale planting projects across New York.

1.7M+ acres suitable for reforestation in NY
111 usable survey responses informed the list
Source: The Nature Conservancy + Ecological Health Network (summary from the PDF)

How to use it (quick)

Use this list alongside a solid site assessment and the best available seed provenance information. The ranking suggests how frequently species may appear across projects—final choices still depend on region, landform, and site conditions.

  • Evaluate site conditions comprehensively
  • Consider provenance/genetic appropriateness
  • Use with other best-practice guidance
